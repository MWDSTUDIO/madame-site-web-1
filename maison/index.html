The Maison

A house of celebration,
at home in the world.

Madame Wedding Design is a luxury full wedding planning service & production house founded by Estelle Bogaert. The maison plans and produces bespoke celebrations worldwide — from intimate private-estate gatherings in Tuscany to multi-day château weekends in France — for couples travelling from the United States, Ireland and beyond, with celebrations beginning at $100,000 and reaching $1M and more.

Featured in seven editions of Vogue, in Martha Stewart Weddings, Brides, Style Me Pretty and Carats & Cake, the maison has been named among the fifteen best wedding planners in Europe. But the work itself is quieter than the accolades: a table set with intention, a landscape allowed to speak, artisans given the time to do their finest work.

Every celebration begins the same way — with a conversation, and the conviction that a wedding is not an event to be managed but an atmosphere to be composed.

We accept a limited number of celebrations each year.
