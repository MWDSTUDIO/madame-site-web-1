The Maison

An atelier of
rare celebrations

Madame Wedding Design is a wedding planning & production house dedicated to a small number of extraordinary weddings each year. Founded by Estelle Bogaert in Paris, the maison conceives every celebration as a singular work — a world of light, texture, music and detail, built entirely around one family.

From first conversation to final candle, we hold every thread: creative direction, planning, and flawless production across France, Italy and Europe.
