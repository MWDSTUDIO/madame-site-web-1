The Maison

Madame Wedding Design is a full wedding planning service & production house dedicated to a small number of extraordinary weddings each year. Founded by Estelle Bogaert, the maison conceives every celebration as a singular work — a world of light, texture, music and detail, built entirely around one family.

From first conversation to final candle, we hold every thread: creative direction, planning, and flawless production — worldwide.
