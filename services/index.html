Questions, answered

Can you plan our wedding abroad, wherever it may be?

Yes — most of the maison's couples live in the United States and plan entirely from abroad. Scouting, tastings and fittings are arranged around your travel, and everything else is carried by the maison, wherever you celebrate.

What scale of celebration do you design?

Celebrations begin at $100,000 and regularly reach $500,000 to $1M and more — from intimate gatherings of thirteen guests on a private Tuscan estate to multi-day château weekends.

How far in advance should we begin?

Twelve to eighteen months for a full destination celebration; the finest estates in Tuscany, Provence and Paris are reserved early. Shorter timelines are occasionally possible — ask.

Which destinations do you know best?

Paris, Provence, Burgundy, Tuscany and the Italian countryside, and Portugal are long-time loves — and the maison plans and produces celebrations worldwide.
