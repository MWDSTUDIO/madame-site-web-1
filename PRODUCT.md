# PRODUCT.md — Madame Wedding Design

## What this is

The public website of Madame Wedding Design — a luxury full wedding planning
service & production house founded by Estelle Bogaert, creating bespoke
celebrations worldwide ($100k to $1M+). The site is the maison's couture
storefront: it must feel like an Aman hotel or a couture house, never like a
wedding-planner template.

## Register

brand — design IS the product. Marketing/portfolio surface. Emotion first.

## Platform

web (static site, vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, deployed on Netlify; Next.js migration planned)

## Audience

UHNW couples (mostly American: New York, Texas, Philadelphia) and refined
modern brides planning destination weddings in Europe. They arrive from
Instagram, press features (Vogue, Martha Stewart Weddings), or AI/Google
search. They are design-literate and allergic to anything generic.

## Voice

Quiet luxury. Restraint, silence, savoir-faire. Short sentences, editorial
(Vogue/AD caliber). Never salesy, never "exclusive"/"luxury" said out loud —
shown, not told. English only on the public site.

## Brand rules (strict)

- Name always "Madame Wedding Design", never "MADAME" alone.
- Specialty: "Full Wedding Planning Service & Production", positioned worldwide.
- No Paris / no city anchor in visible copy (schema.org + contact page only).
- Exactly three services: Full Wedding Planning & Production / Creative
  Direction / Destination & Logistics. Never partial planning or month-of.
- Inquiry budget ranges start at $100,000.

## Design tokens (committed — identity preservation wins)

- Palette: alabaster #F6F3EE (bg), linen #E7E0D5, ink #1E1A17 (text),
  sage #8A8B72 (accent), taupe #A79A88, ink-soft #4A443E. No bright colors, ever.
- Type: Fraunces (display serif, light) + Inter (body/nav, 300). TODO: Canela /
  PP Editorial New when licensed.
- Motion: imperceptible and expensive only — slow fades, 12–16px reveals,
  ~0.9s easing. Never bounce or blink. prefers-reduced-motion respected.
- Imagery: full-bleed film (muted, music on "Enter"), 4:5 portraits, veiled
  in ink (rgba ink ~0.3) for text legibility and mood.

## Anti-references

Typical wedding-planner templates, pastel scripts fonts, Squarespace weddings
themes, bright gradients, badges/cards/shadows, anything that shouts.
